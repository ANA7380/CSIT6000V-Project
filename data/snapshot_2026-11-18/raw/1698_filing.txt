SHENZHEN, China
,
Nov. 17, 2026
/
PRNewswire
/ -- Tencent Music Entertainment Group ("TME," or the "Company") (NYSE: TME and HKEX: 1698), the leading all-in-one music and audio entertainment platform in China, today announced its unaudited financial results for the third quarter ended September 30, 2026.
Third
Quarter 202
6
Financial Highlights
Total revenues
were RMB8.94 billion (US$1.33 billion), representing a 5.6% year-over-year increase.
Revenues from
music related services
[1]
were RMB7.68 billion (US$1.15 billion), representing 10.2% year-over-year growth.
Revenues from membership services
[2]
were RMB4.99 billion (US$744 million), representing 9.2% year-over-year growth.
Revenues from marketing and consumption services
[3]
were RMB2.70 billion (US$402 million), representing 12.1% year-over-year growth, driven by robust growth in revenues from artist-related merchandise and offline performance related services.
On an IFRS basis:
Net profit attributable to equity holders of the Company
was RMB2.15 billion (US$321 million) and remained stable compared with the same period of 2025.
Diluted earnings per ADS
was RMB1.36 (US$0.20), compared with RMB1.38 in the same period of 2025.
On a non-IFRS basis:
Adjusted EBITDA
[4]
was RMB3.02 billion (US$450 million), representing 2.7% year-over-year growth.
Non-IFRS net profit attributable to equity holders of the Company

Non-IFRS diluted earnings per ADS

Total
cash, cash equivalents, term deposits and short-term investments

In the third quarter of 2026, the Company
repurchased
34.7 million ADSs for an aggregate consideration of approximately US$300.0 million in cash.

Against an evolving competitive landscape, we are strengthening our music and audio ecosystem by leveraging our broad IP portfolio and growing pan-IP operating capabilities. Anchored in music, we are bringing IP to life across music, audio, live entertainment, and beyond. Ximalaya further enriches these experiences and creates more ways for users to engage. Each touchpoint reinforces the others, creating richer experiences for users while broadening and extending the reach and longevity of our IP."
Mr. Ross Liang, CEO of TME, continued, "Our platform's strength lies in a consistently enhanced product experience and deep user engagement, which have cultivated a loyal and highly engaged user base that supported healthy SVIP growth and retention. Growing demand for richer pan-IP experiences, now complemented by Ximalaya's offerings, is driving higher spending among both SVIP and other paying members, and we see further opportunity to capture a greater share of
wallet
. Looking ahead, we remain focused on strengthening our value proposition through differentiated content and services to better engage a broader audience."
Third
Quarter 2026 Operational Highlights
IP-Centric Content Ecosystem – Deepening and diversifying our premium music and audio catalogue to serve a broader range of users and listening needs.
Strengthened high-demand music offerings across K-pop, Mandopop, and OSTs to meet evolving listening preferences.
1) Bolstered K-pop catalogue by renewing our contract with HYBE and securing 30-day early release arrangements for selected major releases, including global icon JISOO's latest single. 2) Reinforced Mandopop catalogue, a key driver of music consumption, through our renewed partnership with YH Entertainment Group, securing premium music IP assets and paving the way for future merchandise collaborations. 3) Produced bespoke soundtracks for blockbuster Tencent Video drama series and introduced trending OSTs from popular dramas, including
Against the Current
and
The Early Spring
, further enriching our premium content library.
Broadened our audience reach with a richer selection of
premium audio content spanning entertainment, knowledge, children's, and parenting categories.
1) Collaborated with studios to produce multiple audiobook adaptations of
The Grave Robbers' Chronicles,
bringing fresh appeal to the classic IP and driving stream growth. 2) Expanded our original audio content in popular categories such as history and children's programming, attracting new audience and supporting membership conversion.
Holistic IP Capabilities – Leveraging our distinctive IP operations to amplify artist influence, deepen user engagement, and extend IP value across touchpoints.
Expanded artists' reach through integrated promotion and fan engagement.
1) Advanced collaboration with Weixin Video Accounts by bringing leading artists such as JENNIE, YUQI, Silence Wang, Jane Zhang, and Ziyu
[5]
onto the platform, driving artist-fan interaction and higher average daily video views. 2) Celebrated BIGBANG's 20th anniversary with integrated online campaigns and offline exhibitions across dozens of cities in mainland China, boosting catalogue streams and merchandise sales. 3) Empowered JJ Lin, Lil Ghost and TIA RAY to perform at the Formula 1 Singapore Grand Prix, elevating their global influence.
Unlocked deeper IP value through live concerts, scaling operations from domestic to overseas markets.
1) Staged four stadium concerts for rapper Zhou Yan (GAI), attracting over 120,000 attendees, and hosted concerts for artists and groups such as Will Pan, Shan Yichun, Steven Zhang, and TREASURE. 2) Supported TIA RAY's first world tour across Australia, Canada, Malaysia, and the United States, as well as her performance at the GRAMMY Museum, expanding her international reach. 3) Co-produced the 2026 Pepsi Music Festival, featuring Ashin of Mayday, Jackson Wang, and TransfOrm Project, enhancing Pepsi's brand visibility among younger audiences.
Products & Services – Deepening engagement and driving monetization across everyday listening, premium memberships, and broader IP consumption.
Enhanced user experience across music and audio to reinforce loyalty and broaden user reach.
1) Main music apps: Simplified music discovery through contextualized recommendations and swipe-based feeds, leading to higher daily time spent per user. A major revamp for HarmonyOS Adaptation supported user growth, while our early adaptation for the iPhone Duo earned App Store recognition. 2) Lite product suite: Drove steady user growth through streamlined interfaces, personalized recommendations, and targeted campaigns. 3) Long-form audio: Enriched Ximalaya's pan-audio ecosystem with music and introduced more than 10,000 motion comics to cater to bite-sized entertainment occasions. An upgraded playback interface and AI assistant further improved discovery and personalization.
Enriched benefits to support continued SVIP growth and retention.
1) SVIP recorded another quarter of growth in both paying users and ARPPU, along with improved user retention, driven by expanded fan-centric privileges, premium audio quality, and a growing selection of long-form audio content. 2) Welcomed Fiona Sit to our Fan Club, creating more connections between dedicated fans and their favorite artists. 3) Optimized Ximalaya's VIP offerings with more targeted content and tailored privileges, cultivating a more loyal subscriber base.
Rolled out more pan-IP offerings, driving strong growth in paying users and average spending across IP consumption services. This momentum was supported by solid live performance results and strong fan demand for merchandise spanning artist and literary IP.
Third
Quarter 202
6
Financial Review
Total revenues
increased by RMB478 million, or 5.6%, to RMB8.94 billion (US$1.33 billion) from RMB8.46 billion in the same period of 2025.
Revenues from music related services
increased by 10.2% to RMB7.68 billion (US$1.15 billion), compared with RMB6.97 billion in the same period of 2025. Revenues from membership services were RMB4.99 billion (US$744 million), representing 9.2% year-over-year growth, compared with RMB4.56 billion in the same period of 2025. Revenues from marketing and consumption services were RMB2.70 billion (US$402 million), representing 12.1% year-over-year growth, compared with RMB2.41 billion in the same period of 2025. The increase of revenues from music related services was due to consolidation of Ximalaya, as well as robust growth in revenues from artist-related merchandise sales and offline performance related services, primarily driven by collaborations with Silence Wang, one of our strategic artists. Our SVIP membership program continued to expand and contributed to our membership revenue growth.
Revenues from social entertainment services and others
decreased by 15.7% to RMB1.26 billion (US$188 million) from RMB1.49 billion in the same period of 2025.
Cost of revenues
increased by 7.0% year-over-year to RMB5.12 billion (US$763 million), mainly due to increased costs related to long-form audio content costs and artist-related merchandise, as well as higher costs related to offline performances related services.
Gross margin
was 42.8% compared with 43.5% in the same period of 2025, mainly due to the shift of revenue mix, as revenues from artist-related merchandise and offline performance related services continued to grow, whereas revenues from social entertainment services and others decreased.
Total operating expenses
increased by 16.9% year-over-year to RMB1.53 billion (US$229 million). Operating expenses as a percentage of total revenues increased to 17.1% from 15.5% in the same period of 2025. The increase was primarily due to the consolidation of Ximalaya, including the amortization of intangible assets arising from the acquisition.
On an IFRS basis,
net profit
and
net profit attributable to equity holders of the Company
for the third quarter of 2026 were RMB2.22 billion (US$331 million) and RMB2.15 billion (US$321 million), respectively.
Basic and diluted earnings per American Depositary Shares ("ADS")
for the third quarter of 2026 were RMB1.37 (US$0.20) and RMB1.36 (US$0.20), respectively. The Company had weighted averages of 1.57 billion basic and 1.58 billion diluted ADSs outstanding, respectively. Each ADS represents two of the Company's Class A ordinary shares.
On a non-IFRS basis,
adjusted EBITDA
for the third quarter of 2026 were RMB3.02 billion (US$450 million).
Non-IFRS net profit
was RMB2.53 billion (US$377 million) and
non-IFRS net profit attributable to equity holders of the Company
was RMB2.45 billion (US$365 million).
Non-IFRS basic and diluted earnings per ADS
were RMB1.56 (US$0.23) and RMB1.55 (US$0.23), respectively. Please refer to the section in this press release titled "Non-IFRS Financial Measures" for details.
As of September 30, 2026, the combined balance of the Company's
cash, cash equivalents, term deposits and short-term investments
amounted to RMB51.49 billion (US$7.68 billion), compared with RMB44.22 billion as of June 30, 2026. In September 2026, we issued senior unsecured notes with aggregate principal amount of US$1 billion, consisting of US$500 million due 2031 and US$500 million due 2036.
Share Repurchase Program
Under our previously announced share repurchase program, during the three months ended September 30, 2026, we repurchased a total of 34.7 million ADSs in the open market with cash for an aggregate consideration of approximately US$300.0 million at an average price of US$8.6 per ADS.
Environmental, Social, and Governance ("ESG")
Our commitment to sustainability extends to our physical music products. We have optimized multiple stages of our production process, from the use of eco-friendly materials and resource-efficient packaging to the recycling and reuse of manufacturing materials. To date, these practices have been adopted across a growing portfolio of physical albums and related merchandise.
